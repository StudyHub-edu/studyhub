Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 2, 2026

This policy describes information StudyHub handles when you use the service, why it is used, and the choices available to you. It applies to StudyHub’s website and learning, community, and account features.

1. Information you provide

  • Account and profile: name, email address or phone number, account role, institution, class or level, and profile photo.
  • Things you share: notes, learning resources, questions, messages, reactions, calendar entries, and other content you choose to submit.
  • Support and account requests: information you include when contacting StudyHub.

2. ID verification information

If you start ID verification, your browser uses the camera to capture an image of the card and runs text recognition to identify possible name, institution, class, and expiry details. The captured image and recognized text are submitted to StudyHub for review. The image is stored in a private storage area; the submitted user and authorized administrators can access it for verification. Verification status, review outcome, and any administrator note are also stored.

Do not scan an ID unless you are comfortable sharing it for this purpose and are authorized to do so. StudyHub does not use the camera until you start scanning and grant the browser permission. The OCR result may be inaccurate and is reviewed by an administrator.

3. Information collected through use

StudyHub and its hosting services may process technical information needed to provide and protect the service, such as authentication/session data, feature activity, and basic request or error information. The site also stores preferences such as your selected theme in your browser. Community sign-in may store a session token in browser storage so you do not have to sign in again when opening Community.

4. How information is used

  • To create and secure accounts, sign users in, and show the appropriate StudyHub features.
  • To provide learning, sharing, messaging, calendar, map, and community features.
  • To review ID submissions and prevent misuse, spam, or security issues.
  • To respond to requests and maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the service.

5. What other users can see

Profile details and content may be visible to other users when needed for a feature or when you choose to post in a shared area. Only approved resources are shown as public resources. Private ID images are not public; they are available to the submitting user and authorized administrators. Avoid putting sensitive information in public profiles, messages, or shared content.

6. Service providers and sharing

StudyHub uses service providers to operate features, including Supabase for authentication, database, and file storage. If you choose Google sign-in, Google processes information under its own policies. Map features may contact OpenStreetMap-related services. These providers process information as needed to deliver their services and have their own terms and privacy policies. StudyHub does not sell personal information. Information may also be disclosed when necessary to protect users, comply with law, or respond to a valid legal request.

7. Retention and security

Information is kept while needed to operate the account and features, meet legitimate operational or legal needs, or resolve issues. Retention periods can vary by data type. StudyHub uses access controls intended to limit access to private data, but no online service can guarantee perfect security.

8. Your choices and requests

You can edit profile information in StudyHub and control whether to start an optional ID scan. To ask about accessing, correcting, or deleting account information, contact teamofstudyhub@gmail.com. We may need to verify your request before acting. Some information may need to remain for security, legal, or operational reasons.

9. Children and young users

StudyHub is intended for learning, but online account and privacy rules vary by age and location. If you are below the age at which you can consent to online services where you live, involve a parent or guardian before creating an account or submitting identity information.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as StudyHub changes. We will update the effective date here. Questions or privacy requests can be sent to teamofstudyhub@gmail.com.
